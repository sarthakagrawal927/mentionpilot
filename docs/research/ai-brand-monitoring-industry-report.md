# AI Brand Monitoring & Mention Tracking Industry Report

**Date:** March 17, 2026
**Analyst:** SaaS Maker Market Research

---

## Executive Summary

The AI brand monitoring industry has exploded into a distinct category since mid-2025. What started as a niche concern ("does ChatGPT know my brand exists?") is now a funded, competitive market with 30+ dedicated tools, multiple companies with $20M+ raises, and enterprise customers paying thousands per month. The market sits at the intersection of two mega-trends: (1) traditional brand monitoring / social listening moving to AI-native platforms, and (2) the rise of "Generative Engine Optimization" (GEO) as a new marketing discipline alongside SEO.

**Three key takeaways:**

1. **The AI visibility monitoring market is early but growing fast.** Peec AI hit $4M+ ARR within 10 months of launch and raised $29M total. Scrunch AI has 500+ enterprise customers. Profound raised $20M seed. This is real money chasing a real problem.

2. **Reddit monitoring is a mature but fragmenting market.** GummySearch (140K+ users) shut down in Nov 2025 due to Reddit API policy disputes, creating a vacuum. The remaining tools range from free (F5Bot) to expensive (Brand24 at $79+/mo), with a new wave of AI-powered Reddit lead-gen tools (ReplyAgent, Redreach) blurring the line between monitoring and automated outreach.

3. **There is a clear gap in the market.** AI visibility tools are expensive ($29-$599/mo) and enterprise-focused. Reddit monitoring tools are separate products entirely. Nobody combines "how does AI talk about my brand" with "what are people saying about my brand on Reddit/social" in a single, affordable product aimed at startups and small SaaS companies.

## PART 1: AI Visibility Monitoring Tools

These tools track whether AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, etc.) mention your brand when users ask questions. This is the newer, hotter category.

### How They Work

All tools in this category follow a similar pattern:
1. You define **prompts** (questions users might ask AI that could surface your brand)
2. The tool **runs those prompts** against multiple LLMs on a schedule (daily/weekly)
3. It **analyzes responses** for brand mentions, citations, sentiment, and competitor presence
4. Results are displayed in a **dashboard** with trends over time

The key metrics they track:
- **Brand Mention Rate** -- How often your brand appears in AI responses
- **Share of Voice** -- Your mentions vs. competitors
- **Sentiment** -- Positive/negative/neutral framing
- **Citation Tracking** -- Which URLs the AI cites (yours vs. competitors)
- **Visibility Score** -- Aggregated score across platforms

## PART 2: Reddit & Social Media Monitoring Tools

These tools watch Reddit (and often other platforms) for keyword mentions of your brand. This is a more mature market but currently in flux due to GummySearch's shutdown and Reddit's API policy changes.

### Context: The GummySearch Shutdown

GummySearch, the dominant Reddit monitoring tool with 140,000+ users, shut down commercial operations on November 30, 2025 after failing to reach an agreement with Reddit's Data API policies. This created a massive vacuum and opportunity in the market.

## PART 5: Key Market Observations & Opportunities

### What is Missing in the Market

1. **No unified "AI + Social" monitoring for small teams.** AI visibility tools and Reddit/social monitoring tools are completely separate products. A startup wanting to track both needs 2+ subscriptions ($100-300+/mo combined). Nobody offers a single, affordable product that answers both "what do AI assistants say about my brand?" and "what are real humans saying about my brand on Reddit/social?"

2. **No affordable entry point for startups.** Most AI visibility tools start at $29-100/mo for very limited prompt counts (15-50). Startups and indie hackers need to understand if AI even knows they exist before committing $100+/mo. The free tools (Semrush checker, Trakkr free tier) are one-off checks, not ongoing monitoring.

3. **No lightweight, instant-gratification product.** Every tool requires account creation, brand setup, prompt configuration. Nobody offers a "paste your URL, see results in 30 seconds" experience as a hook into a larger product.

4. **No Chrome extension play.** The entire market is web dashboards. A Chrome extension that shows AI visibility data while you browse competitor sites, your own site, or even while using ChatGPT/Perplexity could be a compelling wedge.

5. **Reddit monitoring is in chaos post-GummySearch.** 140K+ users lost their tool overnight. The alternatives are fragmented across different price points and feature sets. There is room for a clean, focused replacement.

6. **No tool combines monitoring with actionable content recommendations at a low price.** Gauge does this at $599/mo. Nobody does it at $29-49/mo.

The fact that VCs are writing $20M+ checks for seed/Series A in this category signals strong conviction that AI-driven search will displace significant traditional search volume. Gartner forecasts a 30% drop in traditional search volume by 2026.

### Competitive Moats

Most tools in this space have **weak moats**:
- The underlying technology is running prompts against LLM APIs and parsing responses
- No proprietary data advantage (all use the same public LLM APIs)
- Low switching costs (export your prompts, import into another tool)
- Brand/trust is the primary differentiator at the enterprise level

This means a well-executed, affordable product targeting underserved segments (startups, indie hackers, small SaaS) could compete effectively without massive funding.

### Trend Velocity Assessment

- **AI visibility monitoring:** 6-12 months of momentum and accelerating. Not too early, not too late. The enterprise tools are established, but the SMB/startup tier is wide open.
- **Reddit monitoring:** Mature category in transition. Post-GummySearch vacuum creates a timing window.
- **Combined AI + social monitoring:** Essentially zero competitors. Earliest possible timing.

## PART 7: Product Opportunity Analysis

### Opportunity 1: "AI Brand Monitor" for Startups
- **What:** Affordable ($9-29/mo) AI visibility tracking aimed at startups, indie hackers, and small SaaS
- **Why:** Current tools start at $29/mo for 15 prompts (Otterly) or $100/mo (Gauge). The startup segment is unserved.
- **How:** Run prompts against OpenAI/Anthropic/Google APIs, store and trend results, simple dashboard
- **Build Complexity:** Medium (API calls + storage + basic dashboard)
- **Monetization:** Freemium with paid tiers based on prompt count

### Opportunity 2: Combined AI + Reddit/Social Monitor
- **What:** Single product that monitors both AI mentions and Reddit/social mentions
- **Why:** Nobody does this. Teams currently need 2+ tools and 2+ subscriptions.
- **How:** Combine LLM API querying with Reddit API monitoring in one dashboard
- **Build Complexity:** Medium-High (two different data pipelines)
- **Monetization:** $19-49/mo subscriptions

### Opportunity 3: Chrome Extension for AI Visibility
- **What:** Lightweight Chrome extension showing AI visibility data as you browse
- **Why:** Zero competitors in extension form factor. Instant-gratification UX.
- **How:** Extension queries a backend that runs prompts and caches results
- **Build Complexity:** Medium (extension + backend API)
- **Monetization:** Freemium extension, paid for ongoing monitoring

### Opportunity 4: "AI Brand Health" One-Click Audit
